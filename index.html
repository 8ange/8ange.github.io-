01 — Biography

About me

I'm from Kazakhstan 🇰🇿 and currently study in York, UK, where I'm completing an International Year One in Computer Science at the University of York, combining a foundation year and first year into one. In my final year I'll be specialising in Cybersecurity, which has been the plan from the start.

Before university, I spent all 11 years in a private school back home Tamos Education , an international school. In my last year, the school awarded me merit-based discount on tuition after I came back from an International STEM Olympiad in Singapore with 4 Bronze medals. Getting there wasn't just about showing up, there were several selection rounds before even receiving an invitation, so the medals felt earned.

Somewhere along the way I also finished all 10 levels of Mental Arithmetic, which I started as a kid. It taught me more about patience and focus than I realised at the time.

Right now I'm building projects, getting comfortable with new tools, and figuring out what kind of developer I want to be. I'm looking for a summer internship where I can be genuinely useful, ask questions, and learn from people who've been doing this longer than me.

05 — Production Diary

Week 5 · 10 February 2026

This week I worked on my GitHub Pages portfolio. I focused on building and improving the main sections: About, Projects, Skills, and Contact and setting up my CS Maths & Tools page.

I'm most happy with the visual direction. Moving to a navy and beige palette makes the whole site feel more considered and intentional.

I found it difficult to balance the use of italics. I wanted elegance without sacrificing readability, so I was quite deliberate about which elements to style.

Next time I want to improve the responsiveness of the project grid and introduce some subtle animations to make the experience feel more alive.

Week 6 · 17 February 2026

This week I focused on the visual design and styling of the site. I chose a navy and beige colour palette to give the site a professional, editorial feel. I introduced two Google Fonts. Playfair Display for headings and Cormorant Garamond for body text to create a refined typographic hierarchy.

I'm most happy with how the colour scheme came together. The contrast between the dark navy and the warm cream background gives the site a distinctive look that feels intentional rather than generic.

I found it difficult to balance the use of italic styling. I wanted elegance without making the text hard to read, so I was quite deliberate about which elements to italicise.

Next time I want to improve the spacing and layout consistency across all sections.

Week 7 · 27 February 2026

This week I reviewed the site against Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics and identified areas for improvement. I made three key changes: I added a visible confirmation message (toast notification) after the form is submitted so users know their message was received; I improved the navigation by making all anchor links scroll smoothly to their sections and I fixed the mobile layout of the About section so the photo no longer fills the entire screen on small devices.

I'm most happy with the usability improvements overall. Small changes like the toast notification and smooth scrolling make the site feel much more polished and complete.

I found it difficult to identify usability issues with my own site, since I had grown used to how it works. Testing it on a different device helped me notice problems I had missed.

Next time I want to improve accessibility, adding proper alt text to all images and improving keyboard navigation across the site.

Week 8 · 3 March 2026

This week I added the Contact section to the site, including a form with fields for name, email, and message. I also set up a Supabase project and created a table called contact_messages with columns for id, name, email, message, and created_at. I connected the form to Supabase using JavaScript so that submissions are stored directly in the database.

I'm most happy with getting the full end-to-end flow working, a user fills in the form, clicks Send, the data gets stored in Supabase, and a confirmation toast appears on screen. It was satisfying to see it all come together.

I found it difficult to debug the Supabase connection at first. I had an error related to Row Level Security policies, which I resolved by creating an INSERT policy for anonymous users in the Supabase dashboard.

Next time I want to improve the error handling in the form, currently it shows a generic alert if something goes wrong, which isn't a great user experience.

Week 9 · 10 March 2026

This week I worked on improving the overall layout and adding more content to the site. I updated the About section with detailed information about where I'm from Kazakhstan my school background, and my academic journey so far at the University of York.

I'm most happy with how the About section reads now. It feels personal and honest without being too long, and I think it gives a visitor a real sense of who I am.

I found it difficult to decide how much personal detail to include. I wanted the section to be human and engaging, but not overshare.

Next time I want to improve the responsiveness of the layout on smaller screen sizes, particularly the project grid and the about section.

Week 10 · 20 March 2026

This week I added the Achievements section to the site, highlighting three key personal milestones: the International STEM Olympiad in Singapore, the merit-based tuition discount from my school, and completing all 10 levels of Mental Arithmetic. I designed the section as a three-column card grid with a hover effect that inverts the colours.

I'm most happy with the hover interaction on the achievement cards. The dark navy fill on hover feels polished and adds a layer of interactivity that makes the section more engaging.

I found it difficult to write about my own achievements without sounding either too boastful or too modest. I tried to keep the tone factual and honest.

Next time I want to improve the mobile layout of the achievements grid, which currently stacks into a single column.
